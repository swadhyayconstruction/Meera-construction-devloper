Working Experience & Technical Mastery

Deep technical breakdown of how Swadhyay manages every civil construction phase.

1. Soil & Substructure Execution

Topographical soil bearing capacity analysis, deep foundation excavation, anti-termite chemical barrier treatment, and raft/isolated footing casting with heavy grade M25 concrete.

2. Seismic RCC Framing

Fe550D TMT rebar binding following Zone-3/4 seismic detailing standards, machine-batched M25/M30 concrete column and beam pouring, and strict 28-day curing cycles.

3. AAC Block & Brick Masonry

High-thermal-insulation Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) block jointing with polymer mortar, chicken wire mesh reinforcement at concrete joints, and dual-coat damp-proof plastering.

4. Chemical Waterproofing

Elastomeric polymer slurry coating over roof slabs, brick bat coba insulation, and ponding test verification for all wet areas (bathrooms, balconies, terrace).

5. Plumbing & Electrical MEP

CPVC/UPVC multi-layer pressure tested plumbing lines, fire-retardant FRLS copper wiring in concealed conduits, and earth-pit grounding installations.

6. Facade & Architectural Finishings

Weather-shield exterior acrylic emulsion paints, stone veneer cladding, CNC louvers, Italian marble/vitrified tile flooring, and custom ceiling detailings.

Step-By-Step Workflow

Our 7-Step Construction Process

A transparent, stage-by-stage methodology ensuring smooth project progression.

01

Consultation

Understanding plot orientation, budget goals, family preferences, and space requirements.

02

Planning & Survey

Topographical plot measurements, soil bearing capacity testing, and Vastu analysis.

03

2D Floor Plan

Drafting precise room layouts, circulation paths, door/window schedules, and structural columns.

04

3D Exterior Design

Creating photorealistic 3D elevations and texture previews prior to ground breaking.

05

Cost Estimation

Detailed Bill of Quantities (BOQ), material specifications, and milestone payment schedules.

06

On-Site Construction & Framing

Excavation, footing foundation, RCC slab casting, brickwork masonry, plastering, electrical/plumbing conduits, and facade finishing.

07

Handover

Final quality audit, deep cleaning, punch-list resolution, and key handover.

Structural Integrity

Engineering & Quality Standards

We strictly enforce Indian Standard (IS) civil codes across all raw materials and execution phases.

RCC Concrete Mix

Design mix M20/M25 concrete grades with certified slump tests and compulsory 7-day and 28-day cube compression strength testing.

Steel Rebar Grade

Exclusive use of certified Fe550D TMT steel bars for high ductility and superior earthquake resistance in all columns and beams.

Waterproofing Guarantees

Multi-layer chemical elastomeric waterproofing treatment on roof slabs, sunken toilets, and basements to prevent dampness forever.
