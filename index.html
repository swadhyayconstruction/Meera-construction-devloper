How We Work

7-Step Construction Process

A streamlined, transparent timeline ensuring peace of mind from initial idea to key handover.

1
Step 01

Consultation & Needs Assessment

Understanding plot dimensions, budget constraints, design preferences, and lifestyle needs with our civil engineers.

2
Step 02

Site Planning & Surveying

Conducting soil testing, plot orientation check, Vastu layout mapping, and municipal boundary approvals.

3
Step 03

2D Floor Plan Finalization

Drafting accurate architectural floor layouts with optimal room dimensions, ventilation, and space utilization.

4
Step 04

3D Elevation & Photorealistic Renderings

Visualizing exterior textures, lighting, color themes, and materials through full 3D renderings before groundbreaking.

5
Step 05

Quality Check & Material Selection

Selecting certified M25/M30 concrete mixes, Fe550 TMT steel, branded bricks, plumbing, and electrical fixtures.

6
Step 06

On-Site Execution & Civil Construction

Foundation excavation, RCC frame casting, brickwork, plastering, and waterproofing under daily engineer supervision.

7
Step 07

Final Inspection & Handover

Rigorous quality inspection, finishing touches, clean-up, and official key handover for your new home.
